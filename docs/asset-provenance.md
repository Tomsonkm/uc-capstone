# Asset provenance

UC logo images are unmodified downloads from the official [University of Canberra brand toolkit](https://www.canberra.edu.au/about-uc/brand-toolkit), retrieved 9 October 2026.

- Night Blue inline logo: https://www.canberra.edu.au/__data/assets/image/0011/2391248/uc-logo-inline-night-blue-png.png
- White inline logo: https://www.canberra.edu.au/__data/assets/image/0006/2391243/uc-logo-inline-white-png.png
- Roboto and STIX Two Text fonts are served locally from @fontsource packages. Their upstream licensing files are retained in the installed package.
- Interface icons: Lucide, ISC licence.
- Entry-page orbital composition and project artwork: original CSS shapes. No artificial campus photography or generated university logo is used.
